About Us

FAQS

How does EcoCycle work?

Simply sign up, categorize your recyclables, schedule a pickup, and track your contributions—all while earning rewards!

Is EcoCycle free to use?

Yes! Our platform is free to use for individuals. Some services may have premium options for businesses.

What materials can I recycle?

We accept a wide range of recyclables, including paper, plastics, metals, glass, and e-waste.

How do I earn rewards?

You earn points based on the amount and type of recyclables you contribute. Points can be redeemed for discounts, coupons, or donations to environmental causes.

Where is EcoCycle available?

We currently operate in Mumbai. Stay tuned as we expand to more locations!
